# MedCognetics Brand

Apply MedCognetics styling without turning private examples into reusable public
source material. Read [references/brand-style.md](references/brand-style.md)
before styling an artifact.

## Protect the Source Boundary

Classify each input before using it:

- Treat facts recorded in the reference as public website observations.
- Treat format and layout rules in the reference as authored recommendations,
  not official company standards.
- Use user-supplied private content only in its authorized output. Do not copy
  it into this skill, another public repository file, or a public artifact.
- Do not retain private paths, filenames, metadata, wording, figures, or
  artwork as examples.

Confirm the intended audience, destination, and classification when they affect
disclosure. Do not infer that a document is confidential, proprietary, public,
or suitable for regulatory use.

## Handle the Logo

Use the MedCognetics logo only when the user supplies an authorized official
asset or identifies an approved company source.

- Preserve the asset's aspect ratio, colors, and clear background.
- Do not trace, redraw, recolor, crop, stretch, or reconstruct the mark.
- Do not assume that a monochrome, reversed, or co-branded variant is approved.
- Do not copy a website asset into a repository or output merely because it is
  publicly viewable.
- If no authorized asset is available, omit the logo and report the omission.
  Do not typeset the company name as a replacement wordmark.

## Apply the Style

1. Identify the artifact type, audience, destination, classification, output
   format, and authorized assets.
2. Load the public observations and recommendations from the brand reference.
3. Apply the common palette, typography hierarchy, spacing, and image rules.
4. Apply the document or presentation recommendations for the target format.
5. Preserve the source content's meaning and evidence. Do not introduce company
   or product claims from a styling reference.
6. Render the finished artifact and inspect every page or slide.

Keep layouts clean, high-contrast, and easy to scan. Prefer white backgrounds,
blue and charcoal hierarchy, restrained accents, aligned edges, and enough
space around text and figures.

## Handle Classification Markings

Add confidentiality, proprietary, draft, regulatory, or distribution markings
only when an authorized source or the user specifies them. Keep footers minimal
and consistent. Do not copy legal notices or classification text from another
artifact without confirming that they apply.

## Validate the Output

Render the final document or presentation and check:

- clipping, overlap, off-slide objects, and crowded text;
- title, body, caption, and footer hierarchy;
- font substitution and missing glyphs;
- color contrast and meaning that does not rely on color alone;
- image resolution, alignment, and cropping;
- logo authorization, proportions, color, and placement;
- page or slide numbering and classification consistency.

Report which public guidance, format recommendations, and authorized assets
were used. State any omitted logo, unavailable font, unresolved classification,
or rendering limitation.
